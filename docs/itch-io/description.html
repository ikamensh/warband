Warband is a classic fantasy real-time strategy game. Grow a settlement into a fortress, turn a handful of workers into an army, and choose when to defend, expand, or strike. Lead Humans, Orcs, Elves, or Dwarves through story campaigns, skirmishes, and online battles.

Four factions. Find your fighting style.

  • Humans field disciplined infantry, knights, and soaring gryphon riders.
  • Orcs bring hard-hitting grunts, raging ogres, and explosive goblin sappers.
  • Elves strike with long-range rangers, swift stag knights, and forest-walking treants.
  • Dwarves hold the line with bear riders, mortars, and rune golems.

Build your economy, research upgrades, and combine infantry, ranged troops, and siege engines. Enable magic to claim aether rifts and turn a battle with researched spells.

Two campaigns. Eleven missions. Your choices carry forward.

The Undertow descends through the Five Steps of Vesskar Hollow: five missions, all four factions, and ancient locks holding back a power beneath the valley.

The Thornwood War follows a border conflict through six missions of raids, rescues, sieges, and difficult bargains. Choices shape later missions and the ending.

Requirements & first launch

Windows 10/11 (x64) with OpenGL 3.3-compatible graphics drivers, or macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon (M1 or later). Keyboard and mouse or trackpad. Campaigns and skirmishes play offline; online battles need an internet connection.

These Early Access builds are unsigned / not Apple-notarized. Your operating system may ask you to confirm the first launch; see the installation guide for details.
