# Undertow campaign artwork

`warband/assets/campaign/undertow.png` is the campaign's cover illustration,
created with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-14. It shows the Five Steps
of Vesskar Hollow: farmland, saltmarches, the Deep Grove, Tarnlock, and the Maw.
The two figures overlook the shared valley rather than facing one another.

Generation prompt:

> Widescreen background illustration for The Undertow, a fantasy real-time
> strategy campaign. Paint a magnificent terraced basin called Vesskar Hollow:
> five vast steps of inhabited land descend from human farmland and old
> watermills through salt pans and orc herder camps, an elven deep grove,
> dwarf stone-and-brass lockworks, to a cracked crater at the bottom where
> violet aether breathes from the ground. A winding river spills through
> monumental ancient sluice locks down the terraces and glows violet as it
> approaches the crater. One small cloaked human captain and an orc herder
> stand on a high foreground overlook, dwarfed by this shared valley.
> Detailed painterly fantasy strategy-game art, convincing medieval
> engineering, atmospheric depth. Wide 16:9 landscape. Luminous violet and
> cyan below, warm gold fields above, dark teal forest, slate rock. Keep the
> middle relatively low contrast for a translucent menu. No writing, logos,
> UI, watermarks, modern technology, or photorealism.

This is a source asset. Rendered verification frames belong in the Saga
evidence directory and do not replace the artwork.

## Character portraits

The five square dialogue portraits were also generated with the built-in
imagegen tool. Their source files are `warband/assets/campaign/{issa,brakk,
wrenna,dagna,sella}.png`. Each character uses this shared prompt, preceded
by the character description below:

> Square dialogue portrait for The Undertow, a grounded fantasy strategy
> campaign. Head and shoulders, facing slightly three-quarter toward the
> viewer, strong distinctive face, shoulders fully within the square frame.
> Detailed painterly fantasy game illustration with restrained textures,
> warm brass rim light and a faint violet aether reflection, simple dark
> teal atmospheric background. Clear face at a small 104-pixel display.
> Cohesive serious medieval valley setting, natural proportions, no text,
> logos, UI, watermark or modern objects.

- **Issa:** Captain Issa Renn, a human woman in her early thirties, practical
  blue Lockwatch tabard over mail, dark cropped hair and a small old scar at
  the brow. Responsibility and resolve; a brass lock key at the collar.
- **Brakk:** an older broad orc herder and shaman, weathered olive skin, one
  broken tusk, grey-black braided hair, a blue cloth mantle and a medicine
  pouch containing a folded receipt. Quiet dignity, grief without
  melodrama, watchful amber eyes.
- **Wrenna:** an adult elf grove-tender and druid, copper-brown hair woven
  with leaves, moss green robes with a blue keeper sash. Warmly practical
  and faintly wry, violet reflections in her eyes, a root-map medallion.
- **Dagna:** a sturdy older dwarf woman and runepriest lockwright,
  copper-grey braids, dark blue workcoat over bronze rune armour, soot,
  engineering wear, and a small wrench at the collar. Blunt honesty and
  stubborn care, a strong face with deep laugh lines.
- **Sella:** a human woman around sixty, grey hair tied back, red Open Hand
  engineer cloak over plain dark leather and brass fittings. Intelligent,
  composed, tired and compassionate; a geometrical lockwright's pin.
